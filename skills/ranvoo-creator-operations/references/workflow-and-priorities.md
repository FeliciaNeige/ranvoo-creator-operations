# Workflow and priority rules

## Stages

Use the most specific stage supported by evidence: New, Initial Outreach Sent, Awaiting Reply, Interested, Negotiating, Collaboration Agreed, Contract Pending, Address Pending, Sample Pending Shipment, In Transit, Delivered, Brief Sent, Content Pending, Draft Review, Revision Pending, Posting Pending, Published, Payment Pending, Completed, Declined, Termination Candidate, or Terminated.

Never advance a stage from an offer alone.

## Timing

- Fewer than 3 full days without a reply: Monitor.
- At least 3 full days without a reply: Follow-up Due.
- Every follow-up must add a new angle, clarification, proof point, option, or easier next step.
- At least 30 days without a reply: Termination Candidate.
- Never automatically terminate.
- Explicit contract, shipment, content, posting, launch, or payment deadlines override generic timing.

## Priority

1. Blocking: contract, payment, shipping, compliance, or an answer needed to proceed.
2. Due Today: promised or operational deadline due or overdue.
3. Follow-up Due: at least 3 full days without a reply.
4. Monitor: still within an agreed window.
5. Termination Candidate: at least 30 days without a reply.

When email and table conflict, flag the conflict and recommend the smallest correction.

## 更新日期

- When an approved action changes `合作进度` or its equivalent current-stage field, set `更新日期` to today's date in Asia/Shanghai in the same table update.
- Do not change `更新日期` during read-only review or when the progress/current-stage value is unchanged.
- Show the old and new date in the confirmation preview, then verify both the progress value and `更新日期` after writing.
